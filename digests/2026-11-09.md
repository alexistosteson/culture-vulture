# Bay Area Arts, Culture & Curiosities — week of 9 November 2026

Monday 9 November – Sunday 15 November. 455 listings, ordered by remove from
Mountain View.

## Week at a Glance

**This is a punk anniversary week, and the coincidence is almost absurd.** Fifty
years of **Buzzcocks** at the Chapel on Tuesday and Wednesday; fifty years of
**The Damned** at the Fillmore on Wednesday, billed as *Final Damnation 50*, with
the **Flamin' Groovies** — a San Francisco band older still — in support;
**Soft Cell** on a farewell run at the Regency the same night; **L7** at the
Regency on Friday; **KINO**, the Soviet post-punk canon, at the Warfield on
Friday; and on Saturday **The Avengers**, who played the Sex Pistols' last show
in January 1978, on a bill with **Pansy Division** at Bottom of the Hill, which
closes for good at the end of this year. Four of those six are the band that made
the record, not a tribute to it. If you have been meaning to see any of them, the
arithmetic is not going to improve.

**The dominant single booking is The Damned on Wednesday**, and the reason is the
support. Final Damnation 50 with the Flamin' Groovies and Denmark's Courettes is
three generations of the same impulse on one stage in a 1,315-capacity hall, and
nothing else this week has that shape. Against it the same evening:
**Aterciopelados** playing *La Pipa de La Paz* whole for its thirtieth anniversary
at the UC Theatre — sold out, and the best Latin rock booking of the autumn — and
**Jake Xerxes Fussell** with **Dougie Poole** at the Great American. Take The
Damned; take Aterciopelados if you already have tickets.

**455 listings against 420 last week, and the growth is almost all Saturday.**
Saturday the 14th carries 119 listings, the second-busiest day this digest has
recorded. Monday is the quietest at 34. Wednesday is Veterans Day, which puts the
national parks and the Hyde Street Pier ships in free and fills the museums.

**Friday the 13th is the free night, and it is a good one.** The city's official
Diwali celebration, **Bhangra & Beats Night Market**, takes the Great Lawn at
Yerba Buena Gardens from four till nine — free, outdoors, food stalls, and the
single best free thing in the Bay Area this week. Up the hill at the same hour,
**San Francisco Opera and San Francisco Ballet perform free on Fulton Plaza**
under the Civic Center's LED installation, five till eight. Over in SoMa, UNDSCVRD
unveils the **Made With Mahal** mural in the Filipino Cultural District with a
block party round it. Add the **2nd Friday Art Walk** in Jingletown and San
Rafael, **Let's Glow** in downtown San Leandro, and **East Bay Bike Party**, and
Friday costs nothing until you eat.

**Saturday is the day the week turns into a problem.** **Renegade Craft's holiday
fair** puts 250 makers in the Fort Mason Festival Pavilion for five dollars
suggested — the biggest craft fair of the Bay Area year and the fastest way to
finish Christmas. The **de Young**, the **Legion of Honor** and the **Museum of
the African Diaspora** are all free that day, the first two for Bay Area
residents with ID on timed tickets. **Southern Exposure's Monster Drawing Rally**
has a hundred artists drawing in shifts for four hours while you watch. And the
music is stacked: **Bonobo** with a live band and **Sofia Kourtesis** opening at
the Fox; **Rexx Life Raj** playing *Father Figure* ten years on at the UC
Theatre, sold out and a hometown bill; **Allison Russell** at the Fillmore;
**Underoath** at the Masonic; **The Avengers** at Bottom of the Hill, sold out;
**Darren Criss** alone at the Curran; and **David Sedaris** with Susan Orlean at
the opera house. Pick a lane early.

**The two most ambitious new things both open on Saturday and neither is a rock
show.** **Salt & Spirit**, Opera Parallèle's world premiere built on Gullah-Geechee
spirituals, opens the company's seventeenth season on Z Space's Steindler Stage at
half seven, with a second performance Sunday at three. And at Montalvo's Carriage
House, **The Sarod Trilogy** puts **Amjad Ali Khan** — who defined the modern
instrument and is in his eighties — on stage with both his sons. Neither will come
round again in this form.

**The best free music of the week is on Thursday and it is a tribute to a living
institution.** **Juan Atkins Forever** at the Foundry is a free night for the man
who invented Detroit techno — Cybotron, Model 500, the lot. Doors eight, 21+, no
cover. Thursday also has **Ezra Collective** at Bimbo's, sold out and in a
500-capacity room when they could fill four times that, and the first of three
**Stanley Clarke** nights at SFJAZZ with Ron Carter also billed.

**Film is unusually strong and most of it is repertory.** The **29th San Francisco
Transgender Film Festival** — the oldest of its kind anywhere — runs Thursday to
Saturday at the Roxie across six programmes, sliding scale from $5. BAMPFA has
**Yim Soon-rye in person** for *Waikiki Brothers* on Friday and *Little Forest* on
Saturday, plus *Taxi Driver* in the Scorsese retrospective and two Kobayashi
films. The Roxie also has **Ken Russell's *The Devils*** in 4K on Tuesday, *THX
1138* on 35mm the same night, and **Rock Ross on 16mm with the filmmaker present**
on Wednesday. The **30th Arab Film Festival** continues, including a free outdoor
screening at Proxy on Thursday and an Oakland date at the New Parkway on Saturday.

**Two conflicts worth naming.** Wednesday puts The Damned, Aterciopelados and
Fussell within an hour of each other in three different counties — the Berkeley
show is the one you cannot combine with anything. And Saturday at half seven sets
*Salt & Spirit* in the Mission against Rexx Life Raj in Berkeley against The
Avengers in Potrero; all three are the best thing in their category and the
geography makes them mutually exclusive.

**Practical notes.** Bottom of the Hill closes at the end of 2026 and has three
bills this week, two of them sold out — this is the run-out. Downtown Oakland
garage gates shut at seven on weeknights, so take BART to the Fox for Isaiah
Rashad on Thursday and Bonobo on Saturday. The Golden Gate Park bandshell holds
the fog, so **Crucial Reggae Sundays** needs a coat. Filoli's holiday season opens
Saturday with a $10 hillside lot that fills; the Friday **Merry & Bright** preview
is the quieter way in. And the **Safeway Holiday Ice Rink** is up in Union Square
for the season, which is the first unambiguous sign the year is over.
