## 4. Traps — new this session (the 7 October list still applies)

- **Use Addendum 3's `canon()`, not a reconstruction.** The 7 October measurements used an
  unrecorded canonicalizer that did not merge *theatre/theater* or hyphens, which is why its
  counts (27 undeclared, 5 collisions) differ from this session's (29, 4). The measurement
  scripts lived in a scratchpad and are gone; the function in the spec is the record.
- **Matching `sources.yml` by `name:` misses venues declared any other way** — under a series
  name (Music on the Square → *Courthouse Square*), inside a `note:` (Colma), or as a YAML
  comment (Davies). That produced a false positive in each of checks 3 and 4, and is the whole
  reason for `venues:`.
- **Comma-suffixed venue strings defeat canonicalization** (*Spreckels Temple of Music, Golden
  Gate Park*, *Miner Auditorium, SFJAZZ*). Handle by alias, not by stripping after the comma.
- **JS-rendered venue pages can be read here.** Python Playwright is not installed, but
  `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium --headless --no-sandbox --virtual-time-budget=15000 --dump-dom <url>`
  works; it is how The New Parkway's `/upcoming-events` was confirmed live. Whether the
  *weekly run's* fetch tool can render JS is a separate, unanswered question.
- **PR #3 reported `merged: true`** this time, unlike PR #2. Still check ancestry with
  `git merge-base --is-ancestor`, as the 7 October trap says — the flag is not reliable here.
- **The owner answers tersely and per item.** One phrase ("don't block the merge") had two
  readings and cost a round trip; restate the interpretation when an answer could mean two
  things. Present one decision at a time, with options and a stated lean.
