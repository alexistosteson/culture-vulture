# South Bay and Peninsula theatres: what the digest lists and what it misses

18 October 2026. Asked by the owner after the Mountain View Center question.

**How this was done.** The listings column counts every weekly data file from
13 August to 12 October (10 weeks). What each theatre actually programmes was
read from its own site, or its resident companies' sites, on 18 October.
Research agents did that reading; dates marked *unverified* came only from
search results or resale sites.

## The short answer

The digest is not missing these theatres because their sites are hard to read.
It is missing them because **nobody told the weekly run to look**. Most of
what's missed is the **resident companies**: the local opera, symphony and
professional and community theatre companies that use these buildings, whose
own season pages read fine. The theatres the digest does reach, it reaches
through resale-ticket sites, which show the big touring shows and not those
companies.

Separately, **Stanford, Foster City and Half Moon Bay are not in the digest's
list of Peninsula cities**, and that is where the largest single gap is.

## Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts

- **Listed:** 15 performances in 4 of 10 weeks: *The Liver*, *Swan Lake*,
  Teatro Nahual, the Flamenco Society, and TheatreWorks' *Dracula*. All came
  from one resale-ticket site.
- **Missed:** Smuin's *French Kiss* (11 Sep – 18 Oct, *unverified* at this
  venue) and its *Christmas Ballet* (from 21 Nov). The smaller companies that
  rent the hall can't be known: the centre's own site refuses every automated
  reader.
- **Route that works:** TheatreWorks' own season page reads fine. Smuin is
  already among the email subscriptions.

## What it means for the act-now email list

Very little. The email list is for venues the weekly run *can't* read, and most
of these it simply hasn't been pointed at. Only two are unreadable and have a
list: the Hammer Theatre (stale site) and the Mountain View Center, whose main
resident, TheatreWorks, can be read directly anyway.
