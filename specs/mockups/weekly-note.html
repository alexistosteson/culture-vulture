Culture Vulture · Weekly note

The week of 12 October

Monday 12 – Sunday 18 October 2026

The busiest week we have covered: 415 listings, and Saturday alone has 94. Three things stand out.

Most worth doingIf you only go to one thing this week

01
heavens to betsy
Bottom of the Hill · Sat 17 & Sun 18 · $35
Corin Tucker and Tracy Sawyer’s band from before Sleater-Kinney, back for two nights in a 350-person room. It hasn’t happened in thirty years.
02
Market Street Arts & Theater Festival
Mid-Market, San Francisco · Sat 17, 4–11pm · free
Its first year: six blocks, three plazas and more than fifty performances, nearly all free, including BANDALOOP dancing down the side of a building.
03
Friday Nights at OMCA
Oakland Museum of California · Fri 16, 5–9pm · free
Sixty years since the Black Panther Party was founded in Oakland. The museum holds the archive and marks the anniversary, with MacArthur Maze on the garden stage.

Also: Joe Bataan, who invented Latin soul in 1967, plays free in Yerba Buena Gardens on Saturday at 2pm.

Busy or quietWhat is driving the week

Very busy, and back-loaded. The autumn theatre season is fully under way, four acts play several nights each, and three outdoor festivals land on the same Saturday. Monday is the quietest night we have recorded, and that is real: almost everything starts on Thursday. The Peninsula is thin now that the summer series have ended.

What’s missing or uncertainSo you know where to double-check

  • Litquake is mostly missing. The festival is running all week, but its schedule couldn’t be read. Six of its 80-plus events are listed. Check litquake.org.
  • DNA Lounge shows two concerts. That is a floor, not the whole week.
  • SFJAZZ and A.C.T. don’t publish start times we can read. Their shows are listed without times, or once instead of every night.
  • 21 listings are uncertain about a date, time or room. Each one says why in its own description. The Shovels & Rope date at the Guild is the one most likely to be wrong.

Behind this weekFor anyone following how the digest is made

What we couldn’t reach

  • 14 sources refused or returned empty pages this week, among them Litquake’s schedule, DNA Lounge, SFJAZZ, Stanford Live and Cal Performances. Their listings came from other routes, or are missing as noted above.
  • The Roxie’s calendar had to be read twice. The first read silently dropped Saturday.

Changes proposed

  • Record six venues we already list but hadn’t written down waiting for review
  • Start watching Montalvo Arts Center in Saratoga waiting for review
  • Count Half Moon Bay and Stanford as Peninsula waiting for review

Open questions

  • Has Starline Social Club in Oakland reopened? A listing says so, our notes say closed.
  • The SF Opera costume sale was included even though sales are normally left out, because it is closer to a museum than a shop.
